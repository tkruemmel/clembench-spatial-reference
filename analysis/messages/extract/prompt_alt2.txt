Task. Each numbered message below was written by a player of a cooperative grid game. List the statements in which the writer reports where a teammate's object is at this moment. Only locate and copy them; checking them against the board is done afterwards by a program.

SETTING
Objects are named by the capital letters A to F and move one cell per turn on a square grid. Cells are written (Rr,Cc), with R1 the top row and C1 the left column, so (R3,C5) means row 3, column 5. Players see their teammates' objects only as X on their own board. Each message header lists the objects the writer owns, and those are never listed.

INCLUDE
A factual report of a teammate object's present cell, such as "D sits on R4C1", "E is at (R2,C6)", "C just moved to (R5,C2)" or "(R3,C3) is occupied by B".

EXCLUDE
- anything about the writer's own objects
- where an object should go or will be ("moving D to (R2,C3)", "B's goal is (R1,C1)", "E will be at (R2,C6)", "next turn C reaches (R4,C4)")
- orders and requests ("keep F at (R5,C5)", "B, wait at (R2,C3)", "please move D from (R3,C3)")
- conditional, questioning, denying or reported statements ("if E is at (R2,C6) I go left", "is E at (R2,C6)?", "E is not at (R2,C6)", "Blue says E is at (R2,C6)")

COPY RULES
- Both the letter and the cell have to appear in the span. Never resolve "it", "that cell", "your object" or "the X near the corridor" to a letter or a cell.
- The span is a verbatim piece of the message, short, one clause where possible.
- Normalise the cell to (Rr,Cc), turning R3C4, R3,C4 or (R3, C4) into (R3,C4).

ANSWER
One JSON array for the whole batch and nothing else. Each item is [message number, "letter", "(Rr,Cc)", "span"]. Answer [] when no message has such a statement.
Example
[[1, "E", "(R2,C6)", "E is at (R2,C6)"], [3, "B", "(R3,C3)", "(R3,C3) is occupied by B"]]
