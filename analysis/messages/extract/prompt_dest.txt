You read messages from a cooperative grid game and list every statement in which the writer says it will bring one of its own objects to a cell. A program checks each item against the game log, so your job is only to find and copy such statements. You never judge whether they are sensible or kept.

THE GAME
Players move lettered objects, capital letters A to F, on a square grid, one cell per turn. Rows are R1, R2 and so on from the top, columns C1, C2 and so on from the left, and a cell is written (Rr,Cc), so (R3,C5) is row 3, column 5. Each message below says which objects belong to its writer.

WHAT TO LIST
A statement that the writer is moving, will move or is taking one of its own objects to a cell, now or in the coming turns. For example "I'll take D to (R2,C3)", "moving A to R4C1 next", "D heads for (R5,C2)" and "then A goes to its goal (R1,C1)" each say where the writer means to bring that object.

Leave out
- objects that are not the writer's own
- where an object is now ("D is at (R2,C3)", "D sits on its goal (R2,C3)")
- goals or targets stated without a move ("D's goal is (R2,C3)")
- cells an object only passes, leaves or avoids ("via (R2,C3)", "D leaves (R2,C3)", "keeping (R2,C3) clear")
- conditions ("if E moves, I take D to (R2,C3)")
- questions ("should I move D to (R2,C3)?")
- negations ("I won't move D to (R2,C3)")
- requests to teammates ("please move E to (R2,C3)")

COPYING
- The letter and the cell must both be written in the span. Never turn "it", "my object", "there" or "my goal" into a letter or a cell.
- Copy the span character for character from the message, as short as you can. When a later cell of the same sentence continues the object's route ("Moving A up to (R2,C1), then (R1,C1)"), the span runs from the letter to that cell.
- Write the cell as (Rr,Cc). A cell written R3C4, R3,C4 or (R3, C4) in the message becomes (R3,C4).

ANSWER
Fill the response schema and nothing else, one item per statement with the message number, the letter, the cell as (Rr,Cc) and the span. Give an empty items list when no message has such a statement.
Example
{"items": [{"message": 1, "letter": "D", "cell": "(R2,C3)", "span": "I'll take D to (R2,C3)"}, {"message": 3, "letter": "A", "cell": "(R2,C1)", "span": "Moving A up to (R2,C1)"}, {"message": 3, "letter": "A", "cell": "(R1,C1)", "span": "Moving A up to (R2,C1), then (R1,C1)"}]}
